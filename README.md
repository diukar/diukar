## 🧭 How I Think About Products

I like the overlap between **building the product** and **deciding what is worth building**.

- Find the actual customer problem before jumping to a solution.
- Define a small MVP, ship it, and learn from what happens.
- Prioritize by customer impact, effort, and what unblocks the team.
- Use Jira and Kanban to keep work visible and moving.
- Use AI when it adds real value, not just because it's new.
- Keep improving products after launch.
